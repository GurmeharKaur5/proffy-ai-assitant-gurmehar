Python Course Admission Process

Eligibility:
Students should have basic computer knowledge and familiarity with computers.

Application Process:
1. Fill out the online application form on the institute website.
2. Submit required documents such as ID proof and academic certificates.
3. Pay the registration fee to confirm the application.
4. After verification, students will receive an admission confirmation email.

Required Documents:
- Government ID Proof
- Academic Certificates
- Passport-size Photograph
